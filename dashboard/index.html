About
StrikeMatch correlates NASA FIRMS satellite fire detections with ACLED-reported combat events near conflict infrastructure. Every point on this map is a reported strike with an independent thermal signature seen from orbit. Built by artkha1. Data coverage: Feb 2022 onwards, research-tier (~1-year lag).
How the score works

A fire detection and a strike report are matched when they are within 10 km of each other and the fire is observed within 48 hours of the reported event date (+6 h timezone buffer). Each match is scored by multiplying five factors:

score = frp/300 × conf × sources/3 × √(1−d/10km) × (1−|Δt|/54h) × 1000
  • Fire intensity (FRP): radiative power relative to 300 MW (an extreme fire)
  • Detection confidence: VIIRS high (1.0) vs nominal (0.8)
  • Source count: independent outlets reporting the event
  • Proximity: decays to 0 at the 10 km boundary
  • Recency: decays linearly over the time window.

Scores above 20 are strong confirmations (large verified fires); everything shown here clears the archival threshold of 2. Absence of a fire is not absence of a strike — VIIRS cannot see through clouds, and small or brief fires can be missed between satellite passes.

Sensor: VIIRS I-Band 375 m (Suomi-NPP + NOAA-20). Events: ACLED air/drone strikes and shelling/artillery/missile attacks with site-precise coordinates. Full methodology in the README.
